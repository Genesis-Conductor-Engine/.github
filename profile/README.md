## What Genesis Conductor does

Genesis Conductor is an AI-native orchestration and verification platform for high-stakes workflows where AI outputs must be traceable, auditable, and transition-ready.

The platform combines:

- **Agentic orchestration** for multi-agent and A2A-style execution.
- **Provenance verification** for signed workflow artifacts and lineage records.
- **Telemetry and observability** for distributed AI, cloud, edge, and scientific workloads.
- **Thermodynamic AI orchestration** for inference efficiency and resource-aware compute.
- **Federal acquisition intelligence** for agency profiles, saved searches, opportunity triage, and prototype-transition packaging.
